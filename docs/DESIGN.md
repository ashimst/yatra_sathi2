## Brand & Style
The design system channels an architectural sanctuary rooted in high-altitude warmth, earth, and tranquil luxury. Built around an editorial yet deeply tactile aesthetic, it evokes quiet contemplation, organic craftsmanship, and grounding stillness. The visual language blends refined modern minimalism with artisanal textural cues, entirely eradicating clinical, cold, or greenish-gray cast in favor of sun-washed stone, terracotta pottery, sun-baked clay, unbleached linen, and roasted espresso timbers.

This interface serves wellness sanctuaries, meditative hospitality, contemplative publishing, and bespoke artisan platforms. Every interaction communicates physical presence and warmth: surfaces feel like soft linen and warm parchment, text echoes high-craft typography, and focus states evoke the warmth of natural amber sunlight.

## Colors
The palette is explicitly calibrated to eliminate all greenish or cool gray undertones across the entire surface hierarchy. Every hue is infused with warm peach, cream, sunbaked terracotta, or deep espresso:

- **Primary (`#C25932`)**: Sunbaked Terracotta. Used for primary calls-to-action, accent indicators, active indicator rings, and key focal anchors.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Warm Amber. Deployed for subtle highlights, badges, ratings, alert accents, and warm atmospheric highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#FBEFE4`)**: Warm Peach-Terracotta Tint. Used for tonal chips, soft active containers, and subtle contrasting cards.
- **Neutral Primary / Text (`#2A2421`)**: Deep Warm Espresso Charcoal. Replaces cold blacks and neutral grays with rich, roasted timber undertones for headlines, body copy, and high-emphasis icons.
- **Canvas & Surface Levels**:
  - Base Canvas: Warm Cream Linen (`#FBF8F3`).
  - Elevated Container: Soft Sand Beige (`#F5EFEB`).
  - Warm Highlight Card / Sunlit Pod: Subtle Peach Clay (`#FDF6F0`).
  - Recessed / Inset Wells: Light Biscuit Warmth (`#EFE6DE`).
  - Borders & Dividers: Sanded Earth Wire (`#E8DDD3`), strictly warm without any sage, mint, or stone-green cast.

## Typography
The typographic system pairs the literary authority of **Noto Serif** with the organic, legible warmth of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Noto Serif** anchors display headers, page titles, and editorial quotes. Its deliberate curves and chiseled serifs communicate quiet luxury and architectural permanence.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides clarity and friendliness across body copy, navigational controls, metadata, and data tables. Its geometric openness prevents dense text blocks from feeling crowded, complementing the calm breathing room of the layout.
- Microcopy, tags, and eyebrow indicators use small caps or tracked label styles in Plus Jakarta Sans to maintain precision without shouting.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-hybrid architectural grid that prizes generous negative space, allowing imagery and content to breathe:
- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and a maximum bounded layout container of `1280px` framed by `3rem` outer margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` margins.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4-column layout utilizing `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins.

Vertical rhythm relies on balanced multiples of `0.5rem` (8px). Structural breaks and editorial sections utilize `space-xl` (40px) or double-spaced increments (`5rem`) to enforce a calm, deliberate reading pace. Content panels, cards, and inset sheets use internal padding of `space-md` on mobile and `space-lg` on larger displays.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created primarily through warmth-tinted tonal layering and whispered, ambient shadows rather than stark elevation drops. 

1. **Tonal Tiering**: Base canvases sit on `#FBF8F3`. Elevated cards and structural layers rest on `#F5EFEB` or `#FDF6F0`. Raised active states use `#FBEFE4`. 
2. **Warm Ambient Shadows**: Shadows avoid neutral gray and cold black entirely. They use soft, diffused ambient light tinted with Deep Espresso and Terracotta:
   - *Level 1 (Subtle cards, floating badges)*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(194, 89, 50, 0.03)`
   - *Level 2 (Dropdowns, interactive cards on hover)*: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.07), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(194, 89, 50, 0.04)`
   - *Level 3 (Modals, bottom sheets)*: `0 20px 48px -8px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.12), 0 4px 12px rgba(194, 89, 50, 0.06)`
3. **Warm Hairline Borders**: Subtle `1px` borders in `#E8DDD3` create crisp delineation without harsh contrast.

## Shapes
The shape language uses `roundedness: 2`, delivering a balanced curvature that feels organic and handcrafted like smoothed terracotta or natural river stones. 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and minor cards use `0.5rem` (8px) radius.
- Larger containers, editorial panels, and dialog surfaces use `1rem` (16px) radius (`rounded-lg`).
- Feature hero cards and bottom sheets scale up to `1.5rem` (24px) radius (`rounded-xl`).
- Micro-elements such as category tags, badges, and avatars utilize full pill or circular shaping to contrast gently against the architectural rectangles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Sunbaked Terracotta (`#C25932`) background, crisp white/cream text (`#FBF8F3`), `0.5rem` border radius, with a subtle hover transition shifting to `#A84724`.
- **Secondary / Soft**: Subtle peach background (`#FBEFE4`), Espresso charcoal text (`#2A2421`), and a hairline border in `#E8DDD3`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Transparent background with `#2A2421` text, transitioning on hover to a `#F5EFEB` tinted wash.

### Chips & Tags
- Height of 32px with fully rounded (pill) edges.
- Inactive state: `#F5EFEB` background with warm border `#E8DDD3` and `#2A2421` text.
- Active state: Warm Terracotta Tint (`#FBEFE4`) background with `#C25932` border and `#C25932` label.

### Cards
- Surface background set to `#FDF6F0` (warm peach tint) or `#F5EFEB` (soft sand beige).
- Enclosed with a delicate 1px border in `#E8DDD3`.
- Elevated with Level 1 warm ambient shadow. On hover, shifts to Level 2 with a slight +1px negative Y translation.

### Input Fields
- Container filled with `#FBF8F3` (pure warm cream) against `#F5EFEB` panels, framed with 1px border `#E8DDD3`.
- Placeholder text styled in a muted warm espresso tone (`rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.45)`).
- Focus state: Border transitions to `#C25932` accompanied by a warm outer glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(194, 89, 50, 0.15)`. Zero cool blues or cold gray rings.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Default unchecked state: 1.5px border in `#E8DDD3` on an unbleached `#FBF8F3` ground.
- Checked state: Solid `#C25932` fill with white checkmark / inner dot. Focus indicator applies an amber-terracotta glow ring.

### Lists & Navigation Bars
- List items feature generous padding (`space-md`) with soft bottom borders in `#E8DDD3`.
- Navigation bars employ a translucent warm cream background (`rgba(251, 248, 243, 0.92)`) with a gentle backdrop blur (8px) and a bottom divider in `#E8DDD3`, keeping the view luminous, tactile, and grounded.
